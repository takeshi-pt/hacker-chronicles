The error

The remedy

The appropriate fix was to resolve the dependencies with go mod tidy, which creates or updates go.sum. No source rewrite, no bizarre compiler flag and no fictional new framework were needed.

The CTO, who is also the CEO, approved the command immediately after the AI suggested it. The announcement called this a “strategic supply-chain reconciliation”.
