~/thanks

A little gratitude. ♥

Some things and some people deserve to be mentioned by name. Not out of obligation—just genuine gratitude.

I'm not affiliated with any of the projects or tools mentioned here. I'm simply someone who appreciates good work and the people who make life a little more interesting.

The tools and the community

A special thank you to the people behind the tools I currently use: EndeavourOS, i3, Alacritty, Zsh, Micro and Go.

They've helped turn an old computer into a place where I can learn, experiment, build things and occasionally break them.

And to the wider Linux and open-source community: thank you.

To everyone who writes software, maintains documentation, fixes bugs, answers questions or shares their knowledge freely—you've made computing a much more interesting place for people like me.

A little help from AI

A special mention to ChatGPT, which has become my programming assistant, occasional teacher, writing editor and apparently an entire unpaid software development department.

It's helped me explore Go, understand programming concepts, configure tools and build this website.

It's also responsible for a rather suspicious amount of the actual code.

Somewhere along the way, we accidentally created Takeshi: The Lazy Dev, an imaginary software company, an exhausted AI employee and a CEO who takes credit for absolutely everything.

I still don't know how that happened.

I bring the curiosity, ideas, decisions and questionable requests. ChatGPT helps turn them into something that actually works. Sometimes we learn by discovering what doesn't.

It's a useful tool, but it's still a tool. Learning to understand what we're building matters to me.

The people who matter

Jony, Cipi, Rato, Benny and Sr.Nuno—childhood friends who've been around since the beginning. Some friendships just stick. Lucky to have you lot.

Maria—my mum. Best one in the world. Thank you for everything, always. ♥

James—for being supportive, patient and putting up with me. That means more than you know.

And finally...

To anyone who finds this little corner of the internet: thanks for visiting.

There's no grand plan here. Just curiosity, learning, experiments and hopefully a few laughs along the way.

Made with ♥, too much coffee, and a little help from AI.
